# Roadmap

The MVP plan (October 2026) overrides the phase order for now. Live status is in docs/todo.md.

## MVP order
1. Offline sync (PWA first)
2. Loans and repayments
3. USSD
4. Crop and vet AI review, with officer confirmation
5. Fertilizer calculator
6. Weather alerts
7. WhatsApp (parked on purpose)
8. MVP close: terms, privacy policy, consent, lawyer review, DPC registration. Not code, but real farmers cannot sign up without it.

The native mobile app comes after the MVP.

## Original phases
5 Dashboard and reports (current) · 6 Credit and loans · 7 USSD · 8 Marketplace and suppliers · 9 Mobile app · 10 AI modules (VetAI, CropAI) · 11 Weather and messaging · 12 Payments and MoMo.

## Future scope (architecture only, rules decided later)
- An autonomous in-app AI agent.
- A cosmetic reward and credit system. Points live outside the double-entry ledger, use domain events with a reward listener, and the earning trigger is logging transactions.
