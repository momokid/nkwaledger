## Help page
Title: Help

Button: **Take the tour**

Topics (each opens to a few short steps):

1. **Add a farm**
   - Tap My Farm.
   - Tap the button to add a farm.
   - Choose the type of farm and give it a name.
   - Save.
2. **Add a farm unit**
   - Open your farm in My Farm.
   - Tap the button to add a unit (for example a poultry house, a field or a pond).
   - Say how many you have or how big it is.
   - Save. Your agent may need to approve it.
3. **Record money or stock**
   - Tap Record.
   - Pick what happened: sold, bought, lost, or other.
   - Fill in the amount and the date (DD/MM/YYYY).
   - Save. You can see it in My Money.
4. **Use USSD on a phone without internet**
   - Dial [USSD code].
   - The first time, choose a 4-digit PIN.
   - Pick from the menu: your summary, your loans, income, expenses, sales.
   - Press 0 to go back.
5. **Practise USSD with the simulator**
   - [Where the simulator is shown to users: owner to confirm. Today it is a local developer tool only.]
6. **Loans**
   - Tap Credit.
   - See each loan, what it was for, and what is still owed.
   - Mark a purchase "Paid from a loan" when you record it.
   - The loan statement can be printed.
7. **PIN and password**
   - Your PIN is the 4 digits you use on USSD. A password is for the website.
   - Forgot your PIN: at the PIN prompt on USSD enter 0, and your agent will set a temporary PIN.
   - Forgot your password: on the sign-in page, tap "Forgot password".
   - Never tell anyone your PIN or password.
8. **Reports**
   - Tap Reports.
   - Choose the dates you want.
   - Print it or show it to your agent or lender.
9. **Need a person?** – Call or WhatsApp [support number], [hours].

## AI assistant chat window (approved)
- Button (bottom corner of every page): **Ask for help**
- Window title: **Help assistant**
- First message from the bot: "Hello! Ask me how to use NkwaLedger. For example: How do I add a farm?"
- Box hint: "Type your question"
- Send button: **Send**
- Close button: **Close**
- Notice under the box: "Answers come from an AI. Your question is sent to an AI provider. Never type your PIN or password."
- While waiting: "Thinking..."
- After 50 questions in a day: "You have used your 50 questions for today. Please call or WhatsApp {support number}, 24 hours."
- When the AI cannot answer: "Sorry, I cannot answer right now. Please call or WhatsApp {support number}, 24 hours."
- Question is empty or over 500 letters: the Send button stays off, no message.
