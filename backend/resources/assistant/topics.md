# How to
## Add a farm
Tap My Farm. Tap the button to add a farm. Choose the type and give it a name. Save.
## Add a farm unit
Open your farm. Tap the button to add a unit (for example a poultry house, a field or a pond). Say how many or how big. Save. Your agent may need to approve it.
## Record money or stock
Tap Record. Pick what happened (sold, bought, lost, other). Fill in the amount and the date (DD/MM/YYYY). Save. You will see it in My Money.
## Loans
Tap Credit. See each loan, what it was for and what is still owed. Mark a purchase "Paid from a loan" when you record it. The loan statement can be printed.
## PIN and password
The PIN is the 4 digits for USSD, and the password is for the website. Forgot your PIN: enter 0 at the USSD PIN prompt and your agent sets a temporary one. Forgot your password: tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. Never tell anyone either.
## Reports
Tap Reports. Choose the dates. Print it or show it to your agent or lender.
